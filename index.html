Our story

From our counter to yours

“I built the calculator our family shop always needed: fast, accurate, and in the language each jeweller speaks.”

SJ Digital started behind the counter of Saurabh Jewellers in Goa, which has served customers since 1999. Every day brought the same sums: today's rate for each purity, gold given to the karigar and taken back, old gold exchange, girvi interest. One slip could cost money and a customer's trust.

Saurabh Patkar grew up in the shop and turned those daily sums into one simple app. Now we're sharing it with young jewellers, sarafa shops and karigars across India.
