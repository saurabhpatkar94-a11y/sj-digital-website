Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SJ Digital ("we", "us", "our"), a product of Saurabh Jewellers, Goa, India, handles information when you use the SJ Digital – Professional Jewellery Calculator app (the "App") and the website thesjdigital.com (the "Website").

1. Information you enter in the App

The App is a calculator. Values you enter, such as gold rates, weights, purities, karigar names, customer names, notes and design photos, are used to perform calculations and create reports.

  • This information is stored on your device. We do not upload it to our servers.
  • Saved rates, saved reports and design photos stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • When you share or download a report (for example as a PDF or image), you choose where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

2. Camera and photos

If you choose to add a design reference image, the App asks for permission to use your camera or photo library. Photos are used only inside the App on your device. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising

The App may show advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general usage data to show and measure ads, according to their own privacy policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Purchases

Lifetime access is a one-time payment. Payments are handled by the payment provider used at checkout (for example Google Play). We do not receive or store your card or bank details. We may receive basic confirmation of your purchase so that we can provide access and support.

5. The Website

  • The Website does not use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and reliability.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only after you press play. Links to Instagram, YouTube and Google Play take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
  • The Website stores one small value in your browser's session storage to remember that you have already seen the opening splash screen.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and provide support.

7. Children

The App is a business tool intended for jewellers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data security and deletion

Because App data is stored on your device, you control it. You can delete individual records inside the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App to remove all of it. To ask about any information we hold from your emails, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
